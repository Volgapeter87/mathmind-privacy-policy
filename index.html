MathMind Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

MathMind is a brain-training and puzzle application developed by ByteBloom Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use MathMind.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, MathMind may collect or process:

  • Account information, such as email address, when you create or link an account.
  • Age group information used to provide an age-appropriate experience.
  • Game progress, scores, coins, achievements, and leaderboard information.
  • App usage and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising-related information, if advertisements are displayed.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain MathMind.
  • Save your game progress and account.
  • Provide game features, rewards, and leaderboards.
  • Improve app functionality and performance.
  • Display advertisements where permitted.
  • Protect the app against abuse and misuse.

3. Third-Party Services

MathMind may use third-party services, including:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Firestore
  • Google AdMob

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Children and Age-Appropriate Experiences

MathMind may be used by children, teenagers, and adults. The app may ask users to provide their date of birth or select an age group to help provide an appropriate experience.

We aim to handle children's information responsibly and comply with applicable laws and platform policies. Advertising and data collection practices may be restricted for younger users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through MathMind. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the app's services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.
